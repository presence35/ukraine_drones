# Roadmap

Planned-but-not-started features, tracked so a future session can pick them up. Nothing here is
committed to a release.

## Widget / lock-screen glance (#17)

A home-screen widget (or lock-screen glance) showing at a glance: current official alert state for
the focus point, and whether any tracked threat is in the red/yellow zone. Considered a natural
companion to the ongoing monitoring notification; deferred because the notification already covers
the always-on surface and the OS adds widget/lock-screen constraints (provider config, update
cadence, battery/Doze behavior) that deserve a dedicated pass.
